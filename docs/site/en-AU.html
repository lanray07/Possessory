Possessory support

Welcome to Possessory

Create a household, choose its currency and add rooms. Add a belonging with its purchase details and optional warranty expiry date. No account is required.

Your privacy

Inventory records and attachments are stored in the app’s private storage on this device. Nothing is silently synchronised. Uninstalling removes local data, so save and verify a backup first.

Photos, receipts & documents

Attach a receipt image or PDF, choose a page and receipt language, and use the review crop controls or rotation before on-device text extraction. Check every suggestion before applying it. The complete original attachment is retained. Currency changes are never inferred silently.

Backup & restore

In Settings, save a ZIP backup with metadata and attachments. Optionally choose password protection and use at least twelve characters. Keep the password securely: it cannot be recovered. The restore size limit is 100 MiB decompressed; individual attachment imports are limited to 25 MiB. Backup metadata is limited to 8 MiB; attachments are staged in private cache during restore to limit memory use.

Restore

Open a backup in Settings, enter its password if encrypted and inspect the import preview. Possessory checks the schema, attachment hashes and record references. Existing record IDs are retained and only new records are merged. Verify your belongings and attachments after restoring.

Optional reminders

Enable optional reminders in Settings and allow notifications when prompted. Possessory checks upcoming warranty dates and scheduled maintenance approximately daily. Device power management can delay notifications. Reminders do not replace professional safety inspections.

Voice assistant

Use the command phrases shown in the app, then review the transcript. Supported actions include navigation, finding belongings, room counts, creating an item draft, reading warranty reminders and moving one uniquely identified item after confirmation. Expanded actions require Premium. Type a command when device speech recognition is unavailable; the speech provider may use a network service.

Reports

Choose a room, category or currency-specific value filter and an output format. Review serial-number, recorded-value, photo and document-reference settings before saving. Browse the paginated summary or open the PDF preview. Reports are user-maintained inventory records and are not insurer-certified valuations.

Possessory Premium

Premium is a one-time Amazon purchase with no subscription. The store supplies the local price. Purchase and restore require internet access. Amazon user and purchase-receipt identifiers are sent over HTTPS to the Possessory verifier hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which checks Amazon and returns a signed entitlement. Household inventory and photographed receipts are not sent. The verifier uses connection IP addresses and hashed purchase identifiers for short-lived rate limiting, has no customer database and disables application request logging. Cloudflare processes network metadata to operate and secure its service. Offline signed access lasts up to 90 days, or until an earlier verified cancellation takes effect, before it needs refreshing. Existing records, ordinary editing, deletion and backup restore remain available without Premium.

Warranties

Warranty dates and terms are supplied by you. Manufacturer warranties and statutory consumer rights are separate. Possessory does not determine eligibility or provide definitive legal advice.

Delete all local data

Delete a belonging from its detail screen to remove its record, attachments and maintenance entries. To remove every local record and setting, use Delete all local data in Settings and confirm. Files you exported elsewhere are not deleted.

Require device unlock

Enable device-unlock protection in Settings after setting a device PIN, pattern or password. Possessory uses the system credential screen and does not store that credential. Locking hides recent-app previews and screenshots. This is separate from at-rest encryption.

Your privacy

Inventory, household records, room photos, receipts, maintenance documents and preferences are stored in private app storage. No account, advertising or analytics is required. Nothing is silently synchronized. Receipt OCR runs locally in the language you choose. Speech input is optional and may use the device speech provider’s network service. Possessory does not retain raw recordings. Photos, receipts, notes and serial numbers are not sent to the purchase-verification service. When purchases are configured, Amazon user and purchase-receipt identifiers are sent over HTTPS to the Possessory verifier hosted on Cloudflare Workers to check purchases and restore access. The verifier uses the connection IP address and a hash of the purchase identifiers for short-lived rate limiting. It has no customer database, and application request logging is disabled. Cloudflare processes network metadata to operate and secure its service. Amazon separately handles payment. Verified offline access can remain valid for up to 90 days, or until an earlier verified cancellation takes effect, before an online refresh is required. Exports and backups are saved only to a destination you choose; this may be a cloud document provider. Plain exports and ZIP backups are unencrypted. Password-protected backups use AES-GCM; passwords cannot be recovered. Device-unlock protection is optional and does not separately encrypt the database or attachments. Delete individual records or all local data in Settings. Uninstalling removes private records; exported copies remain where you saved them. Keep a verified backup before uninstalling. Contact support about privacy or support-correspondence deletion.
